# Ideal Customer Profile: Enerasoft

> Generated 2026-09-12 | Based on: Enerasoft — AI-first software engineering consultancy (AI agents/automation, DevOps & Cloud, Web/Mobile, Data Engineering), serving SMB and startup clients in Education, SaaS, eCommerce, and Enterprise verticals from Hisar, Haryana, India.

## ICP Summary

Enerasoft's ideal customer is a **funded startup or growth-stage SMB (roughly 10-250 employees)** that needs to ship a software product fast, doesn't have (or can't fully staff) an in-house engineering team capable of building AI features, and is evaluating outsourced development specifically because AI is now a baseline expectation, not a differentiator, among vendors. The market backs this positioning: the global software outsourcing market is trending toward ~$618B in 2026, 47% of startups now outsource development as their primary model (up from ~30% in 2020), and 57% of new outsourcing partnerships are being formed specifically for AI capability — meaning "we use AI internally" is table stakes for winning outsourced work, and Enerasoft's 80% AI-assisted / senior-review workflow is a genuine wedge, not just marketing language.

The best-fit buyer has a concrete product gap (a feature, a platform rebuild, an AI copilot they can't build in-house) and a timeline measured in weeks, not quarters — this is where Enerasoft's "8-week production launch" and "3.2x faster delivery" claims land hardest. The worst-fit buyer is an enterprise with a multi-quarter procurement process and an incumbent systems-integrator relationship, or an idea-stage founder with no budget and no clear scope.

## Technographic Profile

- Already has *some* product in production (not pre-MVP) — Enerasoft's value is speed and AI capability added to an existing codebase or a well-scoped new build, not open-ended R&D

## Buyer Personas

### Persona 1: The Technical Founder/CTO
Runs engineering at a 10-50 person startup, personally reviews vendor code quality, is the economic buyer and champion simultaneously. Cares most about senior-engineer review and test coverage claims (>90% coverage, A+ code quality) because they've been burned by low-quality outsourced code before. Responds to peer-to-peer, specific, metric-led outreach — not generic sales language.

### Persona 2: The VP Engineering at a Growth-Stage SaaS Company
Manages a 20-100 person engineering org, is under board/CEO pressure to ship AI features the roadmap doesn't have staff for. Economic buyer is usually the CEO/CFO but this persona is the champion. Cares about speed-to-production (the "8-week launch" QuoteGuard story) and about augmenting, not replacing, their existing team.

### Persona 3: The Founder/Operator at an eCommerce or Education SMB
Non-technical or semi-technical founder at a 10-100 person company, evaluating outsourced AI/automation to compete with larger players (order orchestration, adaptive learning). Economic buyer and champion in one. Cares most about cost reduction and concrete ROI, not technical depth — lead with the StudyMate/ReviewAI-style outcome metric.
